Mita-Mon Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Mita-Mon (the "App") is an application for recording and later reviewing entertainment and cultural experiences, including movies, anime, manga, books, games, concerts, stage performances, sporting events, exhibitions, and other experiences.

This Privacy Policy explains the information handled by the App, the purposes for which it is used, how it is stored, and communications with external services.

1. Information Handled by the App

Viewing and Experience Records

The App stores information entered by users about viewing and other experiences on the user's device.

This may include titles of works or experiences, dates and periods, locations, ratings, reviews and notes, categories, tags, information about performers, artists, and other people, companions, URLs of related websites, related links, and other information entered by the user.

Most of these fields are optional and are registered through the user's own actions.

Images

The App can store featured images associated with records of works and experiences.

Images may be selected by the user from the device or obtained from web pages specified by the user.

The App does not collect the user's entire photo library and handles only images selected by the user within the App.

Stored images may be converted to a format or size suitable for display in the App before being saved in the App's local storage area on the device.

Web Page Information

When a user enters the URL of a related website and uses the web information retrieval feature, the App may access the specified web page and obtain metadata such as the page title, Open Graph Protocol (OGP) information, Twitter Card information, featured image URLs, and other metadata available on the page.

App Settings

The App may store language settings, display settings, purchase-confirmation status, and other settings required for operation of the App on the user's device.

Purchase and Billing Information

On Android, the App uses Google Play's billing system to offer premium features.

The App may obtain information from Google Play that is necessary for purchase processing, including available product information, prices, purchase status, and ownership status of previously purchased products.

Payment processing is performed by Google Play. The App provider does not directly obtain or store credit card numbers or other payment method details.

The App may temporarily store on the device a confirmation that premium features have been purchased. The final determination of whether premium features are available is based on purchase information obtained from Google Play.

Backup Data

Users can export records stored in the App as a backup file.

A backup file may contain viewing and experience records, categories, tags, person information, related links, other registered information, and images stored within the App.

2. Purposes of Use

Information handled by the App is used to provide App functionality, including recording and displaying experiences, searching and listing past records, calendar views, organizing records by ratings and categories, displaying images, retrieving information from related web pages, backup and restoration, saving App settings, processing purchases through Google Play, checking purchase status, restoring purchased features, determining whether premium features are available, and providing other App functions.

The App provider does not use this information for advertising or behavioral tracking.

3. Data Storage

Major user data, including viewing and experience records, settings, and images saved within the App, is generally stored locally on the user's device.

The App currently does not provide account or cloud synchronization functionality that uploads and stores users' viewing records, reviews, images, or similar data on servers operated by the App provider.

On Android, App data is excluded from Android's cloud Auto Backup.

However, on Android 12 and later, Android's device-to-device transfer functionality may transfer the database containing viewing and experience records, App settings, and certain images stored by the App to a new device during a device migration or similar process.

Such device-to-device transfer is provided by the Android operating system. The privacy policies of Android, the device provider, or other providers involved in that functionality may apply to data handled through the transfer.

4. Communications with External Services and Websites

Retrieving Information from Web Pages

When a user uses the feature for retrieving information from a web page, the App communicates directly with the URL entered by the user.

As part of this communication, the destination website or service provider may receive or record information normally associated with web communications, including the user's IP address, access date and time, information about the device or application used for the communication, the requested URL, and similar information.

The App analyzes the retrieved page to obtain information such as the title, description, OGP metadata, and image URLs in order to assist the user in creating records.

Because the destination depends on the URL entered by the user, these communications are not limited to any particular external service.

Please refer to the privacy policy of each website or service provider for information on how that website or service handles data.

Retrieving External Images

When the App displays or saves an image available on the internet, such as an image URL obtained from a web page, it communicates with the server that provides the image.

The image provider may receive or record the user's IP address, access date and time, and other information normally associated with web communications.

Billing through Google Play

On Android, the App uses Google Play's billing system for purchases of premium features.

The App communicates with Google Play to retrieve product information, process purchases, check purchase status, and restore previously purchased features.

Payments and purchase information handled through Google Play are subject to Google's privacy policy and other applicable terms.

The App provider does not directly obtain or store credit card numbers or other payment method details.

Related Links

When a user opens a related link registered in the App, the App may use the device operating system to launch a web browser or another external application.

After a link is opened, the handling of data is governed by the privacy policies of the destination service and the browser or other application being used.

File Sharing and File Selection

When exporting or importing backup files or selecting images, the App may use file selection, image selection, file sharing, and similar functionality provided by the device operating system.

If the user selects cloud storage, email, a messaging application, or another external service as the save or sharing destination, subsequent handling of the data is governed by the privacy policy of the selected service.

5. Images

The App may store images associated with records in the App's local storage area on the device.

When the user selects an image from the device, the App may read the selected image, convert it into a format suitable for use in the App, and save the converted image.

When an image is obtained from a web page, the App may save the image on the device and subsequently prefer the locally saved copy for display.

Copyright and other rights in original images and images available on websites remain with the respective rights holders.

6. Backup Files

The App allows users to create backup files, such as ZIP archives, containing record data and saved images.

A backup file may contain titles, viewing dates, locations, reviews, ratings, categories, tags, performer or artist information, companions, related links, saved images, and other information registered in the App.

Backup files are created, saved, and shared through the user's own actions.

The App currently does not provide its own password protection or encryption for backup files.

Users are therefore responsible for appropriately managing where backup files are stored and with whom they are shared.

A backup file exported from the App is not automatically deleted when records are deleted from the App or when the App is uninstalled.

7. Disclosure of Information to Third Parties

The App provider does not sell viewing and experience records, reviews, images, or other information registered by users in the App to third parties.

The App provider also does not provide such information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

This does not apply where the user chooses to save or share data through an external service, where communication with a website specified by the user is required, where communications are required for Google Play purchase processing or purchase-status verification, where disclosure is required by law, or where other external communications are necessary to provide App functionality.

8. Advertising and Analytics

The App currently does not include advertising functionality.

The App also does not use advertising SDKs for behavioral tracking, analytics services intended to analyze user behavior, or analytics services intended to identify individual users.

If such functionality is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

9. Retention and Deletion of Data

Viewing and experience records and related images remain stored on the device until deleted by the user.

Users can delete individual records from within the App.

Users can also delete locally stored App data through the App information screen provided by Android or another operating system, or by uninstalling the App.

The locally stored confirmation of premium purchase status may also be deleted when App data is removed. However, the purchase entitlement itself is managed by Google Play, and the App will check the purchase status again through Google Play after reinstallation or in similar situations.

Backup files exported from the App cannot be automatically deleted by the App. Users should delete such files themselves when they are no longer needed.

Because the App provider does not store users' viewing and experience records on servers operated by the provider, there are currently no such server-side records for the provider to delete.

Data transferred through Android's device-to-device transfer functionality or stored by the user in cloud storage or other external services may need to be deleted separately from those services or devices.

10. Security

The App stores major user data, including viewing and experience records and saved images, in App-specific storage areas on the device and uses sandboxing and other security features provided by the operating system.

When communicating with external websites or services, HTTPS is used when the destination supports HTTPS.

However, complete security of internet communications and data stored on a device cannot be guaranteed.

In particular, exported backup files may contain information and images registered by the user in the App and should be managed appropriately.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for the purpose of intentionally collecting personal information from children.

The target age and other handling of the App are governed by information provided on Google Play and other app stores, applicable laws and regulations, and the policies of the relevant app stores.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be changed in connection with additions to App functionality, changes to storage or external communication methods, the addition of external services, changes in laws or regulations, or changes to policies of Google Play or other service providers.

If an important change is made, notice will be provided within the App or on the web page where this Privacy Policy is published, as appropriate.
