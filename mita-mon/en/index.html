EASY TO RECORD

Easy to record, your way.

New entry

Make a quick note.
Add more detail when you want.

You don't need to fill in everything every time. Start with the essentials, then add more whenever you like.

  • Record a single date or a date range
  • Add categories, tags, artists, and companions
  • Customize categories to fit the way you use the app
  • Add a five-star rating, review, notes, and location
  • Log another viewing of a work you've already recorded
