Tile Trails Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Tile Trails (the “App”) records activities such as walking, running, cycling, and driving so users can review their routes and visited hexagonal tiles on a map. This Policy explains the information handled by the App and its communications with external services, including Google Maps.

You do not need to create an account to use the App.

1. Information Handled by the App

Activity records may include session start and end times, activity type, title, paused time, and GPS location data (latitude, longitude, timestamp, and location accuracy). The App derives information such as distance, active duration, speed, and visited hexagonal H3 tiles from these records. A session title may be generated from the place name of the first recorded location and can be edited by the user.

The App also stores settings on the device, such as language, distance units, automatic stop, and map display mode. The App does not collect your name, email address, or contacts simply because you use it.

2. Location Use and Permissions

When you start an activity session, the App uses location data to record your route and visited tiles. Location may continue to be collected while the App is in the background during an active recording. Location recording stops when you pause the session and ends when you finish it. The operating system’s precise-location and background-location permissions are required for this feature.

When you use the map’s current-location control outside an active recording, the App may obtain your current location to center the map. That location is not saved as a track point. The activity-recording feature cannot work without location permission.

3. Purposes of Use

Information in the App is used to record activities; display routes and maps; show history and statistics; calculate visited tiles; edit and delete records; and create or restore backups. The App provider does not use activity records for advertising or behavioral tracking.

4. Storage and Server Communications

Activity records and location data are stored in an SQLite database on your device. Settings are stored in the App’s local settings storage. The App has no account system, user-data server operated by the App provider, or cloud-sync feature. During normal use, the App does not automatically send activity records or GPS history to the App provider, and the provider does not operate a server to receive or store them.

On Android, the App disables Android cloud Auto Backup. Device-transfer features provided by an operating system or device manufacturer are subject to that provider’s functionality and policies.

5. Google Maps and External Services

The App uses the Google Maps SDK to display maps, which requires communication between your device and Google Maps servers. In connection with these requests, Google may receive or record your IP address, device and operating-system information, App and SDK versions, request times, and map interaction information. Google’s published Android SDK materials also describe an SDK identifier, crash information, and interactions such as panning or zooming. Map requests may indicate the area being displayed; while a recording is active, if the map follows your current location, Google may be able to infer your approximate area. See Google’s Maps SDK data disclosure and Google Maps Platform data handling information for details.

The App draws route and visited-tile overlays using records stored on your device. It does not send your complete activity history to a server operated by the App provider. However, Google may process the map area and other information associated with SDK requests to display the map. Google’s handling of information is governed by, among other things, Google’s Privacy Policy.

When creating a session title from the first GPS location, the App uses the reverse-geocoding feature provided by the operating system. Depending on your device and operating system, the coordinates used to obtain a place name may be sent to the operating-system provider or another service provider. Their privacy policies apply to that processing.

6. Backup and Restore

At your request, completed sessions can be exported as a ZIP backup and imported later. A backup may include session dates and times, activity type, title, paused time, GPS coordinates, timestamps, and location accuracy.

The backup is saved to a location you choose through the operating system’s save or share features. The App does not send it automatically. Because a backup contains location history, store it securely and take care when sharing it. If you choose cloud storage or another app as the destination, that service’s privacy policy applies.

7. Disclosure to Third Parties

The App provider does not sell activity records stored on your device or provide them to third parties for advertising. Providers of Google Maps SDK, operating-system location and reverse-geocoding features, and any backup or sharing service you choose may handle information needed to provide those features. Except as required by law, the App provider does not independently disclose your user data to third parties.

The App does not include an App-provider advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. Information handled by Google Maps SDK to operate its map service is described above.

8. Retention and Deletion

Activity records remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual sessions in the App. You can also erase local App data through your operating system’s app settings or uninstall the App.

Backup files cannot be deleted by the App; delete them from their storage location yourself. Normal use of the App does not create activity records stored on a server operated by the App provider. Logs or data held by Google, the operating system, or a service you choose are subject to that provider’s procedures.

9. Security

The App stores records in an App-specific area on your device and uses security features provided by the operating system. Complete security of a device or network communication cannot be guaranteed. In particular, backup files contain location history, so choose storage locations and recipients carefully.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated if the App’s features, data practices, or external services change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
